## Layout summary (8 lines)

- Corpus: 12 OA main-text figures from the specified journals; the selected papers cover working memory, attention, inhibition, Stroop conflict, timing, learning, reinforcement, and sleep-related memory tasks.
- Condition/group small multiples of TFRs: **7** — Baykan 2024 Fig. 7; Stolz 2023 Fig. 3; Murray 2025 Fig. 5; Lum 2023 Fig. 5; Somon 2023 Fig. 5; Thiele 2024 Fig. 3; Abdellahi 2023 Fig. 2.
- ROI TFR paired with scalp topographies: **3** — Ziri 2024 Fig. 5; Haciahmet 2023 Fig. 4; Klaassen 2024 Fig. 2.
- Multi-view/band decomposition layouts: **2** — Rawls 2025 Fig. 2 (band/time topographies plus selected-sensor TFR surfaces); Frelih 2025 Fig. 3 (raw, corrected, periodic, and aperiodic views).
- Baseline/unit: no single exact window is visible across captions. Explicit windows include −300 to −200 ms (Stolz), −300 to −100 ms (Murray), −500 to −200 ms (Frelih), and −2 to 0 s (Thiele). dB is the most frequently explicit power unit; percentage change and uncorrected/raw activity also occur.
- Frequency: explicitly exposed plotted/analysed ranges span about 1–50 Hz; theta and alpha recur most, with beta included in several broad maps.
- Colour: captions rarely name a palette or colorbar placement. Somon explicitly uses red for higher and blue for lower relative power; the rest are n/a in article-page text.
- Topomaps: when present, they accompany ROI TFRs as a lower row or neighboring panel, or summarize selected time-frequency bands/windows; Rawls pairs them with per-band selected-sensor TFR surfaces.
