Fields use `n/a` when the article page/figure legend/methods did not state the item. Journal scope was broadened beyond the named journals where needed to reach 12 papers. This is a layout survey, not a comparison of localization validity.

## Layout summary

- Time-resolved cortical snapshots split by stimulus condition/contrast: **4 papers** (Wang et al.; You et al.; Das et al.; Park & Gross).
- Surface source maps paired with ERP, sensor-space or statistical panels: **4 papers** (Vahid et al.; Simonet et al.; Momi et al.; Zhang et al.).
- 3D or orthogonal brain views emphasizing selected time/contrast effects: **2 papers** (McCracken et al.; Soshi).
- Most often reported inverse family: minimum-norm variants (MNE/dSPM) or sLORETA; SNR/lambda²/loose/depth were inconsistently reported. sLORETA examples used 5-mm, 6,239-voxel grids (Vahid; Simonet; Soshi); surface models included fsaverage (Wang; Zhang) and individual anatomy (You; Das). Views usually showed bilateral cortical surfaces; slices/3D volume views appeared in Vahid and McCracken.
- Thresholding ranged from fixed dSPM cutoffs (Wang) to permutation/cluster-corrected maps (You; Simonet; Soshi; Park & Gross); several legends omit scale limits and color-bar placement. ROI time courses were explicitly adjacent in **2/12** (You; Das); labels varied by paper.
- Corpus includes **at least 8** language or cognitive ERP/task studies (Vahid; Simonet; Wang; You; Soshi; Jagannathan; Das; Park & Gross); rows marked MEG identify MEG-only or mixed recordings.
